Approved languages: cs, en, de, fr, it, es, pl, nl, ro, hu, sk, sl. Portuguese is excluded. Slovak and Slovenian are separate languages.

## Owner-confirmed mechanical meanings

- Front/rear of the binding always refer to the boot: front = toe end, rear = heel end. Turning the base for snowshoe mode does not change these labels.
- Keyhole: keyhole-shaped mounting slot in the binding, not a door lock. Introduce its shape and purpose; subsequently use front/rear mounting slot consistently.
- Pin: mounting pin on the base. In snowshoe mode attach the front mounting slot to the middle pin on each side. The front pin is unused; the heel can move slightly.
- Mini-ski mode: attach rear mounting slot first, then front mounting slot with the release button. Both ends are secured, on both sides.
- Textile heel strap: sets boot size and position. Separate from both ratchet straps.
- Front ratchet strap: over the toe of the boot.
- Rear ratchet strap: over the instep of the boot.
- Upper strap/cuff: hook-and-loop strap around the shin. It can sit over the boot or around the leg above the boot; do not imply that bare-skin contact is required.
- Snowshoe fastening order: front ratchet strap, rear ratchet strap, upper hook-and-loop strap.
- Snowshoe boot position: align boot toe with the forward, metal-spiked end of Combo Ski, using the textile heel strap.
- Stance: feet close together, knees bent, maintain fore-and-aft balance. Do not advise leaning backwards.
- One foot ahead of the other lengthens the base of support front-to-back, not sideways.
- Hockey stop: turn both mini-skis sideways relative to travel and brake by sliding on the edges, as on ice skates. Do not translate as snowplough braking.
- Backpack: position the product so the metal teeth cannot scratch the sliding surfaces. Do not retain the old absolute instruction to put sliding surfaces together.
